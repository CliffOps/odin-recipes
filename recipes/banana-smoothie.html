Banana Smoothie

← Back to Recipes

Description

This creamy and naturally sweet banana smoothie makes a quick, wholesome breakfast or an energizing afternoon treat.

Made with just a few pantry staples, it blends into a thick, refreshing beverage in under five minutes.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas (preferably frozen for extra thickness)
  • 1 cup milk (dairy, almond, or oat)
  • ½ cup plain or vanilla Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • ½ cup ice cubes
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

Steps

  1. Peel the bananas and slice them into chunks.
  2. Add the banana slices, milk, Greek yogurt, honey, vanilla extract, and ice cubes into a blender.
  3. Cover and blend on high speed for 30 to 60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy.
  4. Pour into a tall glass and garnish with an extra slice of banana if desired.

Enjoy your smoothie!
